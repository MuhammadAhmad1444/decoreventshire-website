Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

What we collect

When you send a quote request, contact form or sign up for offers, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, phone, email, event details and venue address.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, deliver your booking and, if you opted in, send occasional offers. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Storage & sharing

Your information is stored securely and is never sold. We only share details with service providers needed to run our business, such as email hosting.

Your quote cart

Items in your quote cart are stored in your own browser so they're there when you come back. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Access & questions

To access or correct your information, contact us on +61 420 474 372 or info@decoreventshire.com.au.
